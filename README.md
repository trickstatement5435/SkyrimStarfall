# Starfall Research Site (Gravity Gun expansion)

A Lambda Directorate research facility inside a meteor crater near Riverwood, with scientists, Combine-armored security, a short quest ("Anomalous Materials"), and zombies plus headcrabs that start turning up all over Skyrim.

Nothing in the Gravity Gun mod is changed. This expansion sits beside it.

## What's in the box

| Piece | What it does | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| `StarfallCreatures.esp` (ESL flagged) | Zombies (classic, fast, poison, zombified researcher) on the draugr skeleton, headcrabs (classic, fast, poison) on the skeever skeleton, factions, leveled lists, crashed canister | Skyrim.esm only. Works without the Gravity Gun. |
| `StarfallSite.esp` | Crater worldspace, facility interior, sealed Containment Wing B, 7 scientists, Captain Moran and 17 guards, ~500 lines of dialogue, quest, 13 lore notes, stun baton, lab coat, Directorate armor | Skyrim.esm, **GravityGun.esp**, StarfallCreatures.esp |
| `SKSE/Plugins/StarfallSite.dll` | World spawner, headcrab pounce, NPC chatter, quest actions, gate placement and lock | Build it with the GitHub workflow (below). SKSE64 + Address Library. |
| `SKSE/Plugins/StarfallSite/forms.json` | Data table the DLL reads (generated, do not hand edit ids) | |
| `SKSE/Plugins/StarfallSite.ini` | Tuning: spawn chances and caps, pounce, chatter, gate position | |

**Load order:** GravityGun.esp, then StarfallCreatures.esp, then StarfallSite.esp.

## Install (MO2)

1. Build the DLL: copy `.github/workflows/starfall.yml` into your repo's `.github/workflows/` (it's in this zip at that path), push, then run **Actions > Build StarfallSite.dll > Run workflow** and download the `StarfallSite-dll` artifact.
2. Make a new MO2 mod (for example "Starfall Research Site") and put the contents of `Facility/mod/` in it.
3. Add the DLL from the artifact to `SKSE/Plugins/` in that mod. The INI is already in the mod folder; ignore the sample json in the artifact.
4. Enable the mod, check the load order above, and launch through SKSE.

## How it plays

- **The gate.** There's a Directorate checkpoint near Embershard Mine (map marker "Starfall Crater").
  - Without the Gravity Gun in your inventory, the sentry turns you away and the gate stays locked.
  - With it, the gate opens and you walk down the canyon to the crater.
- **The crater.** It has a guard checkpoint, four watch towers, a perimeter patrol, a camp on the east plains, the facility entrance bunker and a crashed headcrab canister out west. That last one is an "escaped specimen" encounter.
- **The facility.** It has a lobby security checkpoint, Kast's office, Lab A (Hale), the medical lab (Rusk), quarters, the mess, engineering (Venn), the observation room with a window into the test chamber, storage and the armory, and the containment wing.
  - Wing B is behind a keycard door, dark and overrun.
- **People.** Scientists sleep in the quarters, eat in the mess at noon and 6pm, and work in their labs the rest of the day. Guards hold posts or patrol. Pairs of NPCs chat with each other when you're near (35 conversations, some depend on quest progress).
- **The quest, "Anomalous Materials".** It starts when you first enter the site carrying the Gravity Gun.
  1. Talk to Director Kast.
  2. Bring Hale 3 Energy Crystals.
  3. Rusk gives you the Wing B keycard.
  4. Recover his journal.
  5. Confront Kast.
  - Reward: Directorate clearance, which makes Hale a vendor (crystals, batons, lab coats) and opens the armory locker.
- **Out in Skyrim.** When you enter a dungeon, cave, ruin or wilderness cell, there's a small chance a group of headcrabs and/or zombies is seeded there, sometimes with a crashed canister. Groups scale with your level.
  - Never within 1500 units of you.
  - Max 10 alive at once.
  - Each cell has a 72-hour cooldown.
  - Bodies are cleaned up after a day.
  - Towns, inns and player homes are skipped.
  - Everything is tunable in the INI.

## Not tested in game (please check these first)

I can't run Skyrim or the Creation Kit here. Everything was generated by script, then structurally checked:
- every record and subrecord against xEdit's definitions
- every FormID reference
- every mesh and texture path
- navmesh topology
- terrain seams
- a C++ check of the DLL against the CommonLib headers

An independent review pass found 14 problems, and they're fixed. The things most likely to need a second pass:

1. **The gate position is approximate.** It's placed next to the Embershard Mine map marker (offset 900, -600) and snapped to the ground. If it lands somewhere ugly, stand where you want it, type `player.getpos x`, `player.getpos y`, `player.getpos z` and `player.getangle z` in the console, and put the numbers in `StarfallSite.ini` under `[Gate]`.
2. **NPC pathing (navmesh).** The navmesh is hand-generated (64-unit grid inside, 128 outside), with links across cells and through the load doors. If NPCs stand frozen or walk into walls, tell me. The likely cause is triangle winding, which is a one-line flip.
3. **The Skyrim-side gate pieces** are injected into Tamriel's persistent cell through "partial form" overrides (no vanilla data copied). If the gate never appears, the DLL log will say so (`Documents/My Games/Skyrim Special Edition/SKSE/StarfallSite.log`).
4. **Creature fit.** Classic, fast and lab-coat zombies fit the draugr skeleton well. The poison zombie was hunched in the source and is straightened, so expect some stretching around its back crabs. Headcrabs ride skeever animations, scaled to about half size.
5. **Headcrab pounce** is experimental. Turn it off with `EnablePounce = 0` if it misbehaves.
6. **Faces.** NPCs have no FaceGen data. Heads are covered by the HL head / Combine helmet armor, so this shouldn't show, but a gray-face bug would come from this.

## Quick test (console)

```
coc SFFacilityInterior          ; facility lobby
coc SFWingBInterior             ; sealed Wing B
cow SFCraterWorld 0 -1          ; crater floor, near the facility bunker
help "Zombie" 4                 ; lists the zombie NPC ids, then: player.placeatme <id> 1
help "Headcrab" 4
sqs SFQuestMain                 ; quest stages
setstage SFQuestMain 10
```

Set `DebugLog = 1` in the INI to see every spawner, chatter, gate and dialogue decision in `StarfallSite.log`.

## Voice files

See `VOICE.md` and `voice_manifest.csv`: one row per line, with the exact folder and file name to drop your AI-generated audio into. Until then, NPCs show subtitles.

## Rebuilding from source (`Facility/tools`)

`build_creatures.py`, then `build_site.py`, regenerate both plugins plus forms.json and the voice manifest. They read the layouts (`site_interior.py`, `site_exterior.py`), the dialogue (`Facility/dialogue/*.json`), the prop manifest, and vanilla FormIDs (`Facility/vanilla/formids.json`). Mesh tools:
- `make_guard.py`, `make_scientist.py`, `make_zombies.py`, `make_zombie_scientist.py` and `make_headcrabs.py` need your source assets and the vanilla skeletons in `src_assets/` and `vanilla/`.
- The `kit/` scripts build the props.
